Farm Alarm Text Alerts

Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how Pham's Chickens LLC ("we", "us") handles personal information collected for our farm alarm text alert program, Pham's Chickens Alerts. The program sends automated operational alerts to our employees, farm managers, and other authorized personnel who have signed a consent form.

2. Information we collect

For the alert program we collect your name, your mobile phone number, and your signed consent form, including the date you signed it. Our messaging provider also records delivery information for each message, such as the time it was sent, whether it was delivered, and any reply you send, including STOP and HELP.

3. How we use it

We use this information only to send farm alarm and system alerts, to confirm your enrollment, to honor opt-out and help requests, to keep a record of consent, and to troubleshoot message delivery. We do not use it for marketing or advertising.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, text message opt-in data, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your mobile number is passed to the service providers that deliver our messages, which are our SMS messaging provider and the wireless carriers, solely so the alerts can be delivered. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any other third parties.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your number on the alert list until you opt out, ask to be removed, or no longer work with the farm. We keep signed consent forms and opt-out records for as long as needed to show that messages were sent with permission.

6. Security

Access to the alert list and consent forms is limited to farm administrators. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect this information.

7. Your choices

Reply STOP to any alert to stop receiving messages, or ask a farm administrator to remove your number. Reply HELP for help. You can also ask us to correct your number or tell you what information we hold about you for this program. See the SMS Terms and Conditions for more about the program.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always posted on this page with its effective date.
