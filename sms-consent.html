Farm Alarm Text Alerts

Text Alert Consent Form

Pham's Chickens LLC uses an automated monitoring system in its poultry houses. When the system detects a problem, such as high or low temperature, a power failure, or a water or ventilation fault, it sends a text message to the employees and farm managers responsible for responding. This form records your agreement to receive those alerts on your mobile phone.

What you are agreeing to

  • Who is texting you: Pham's Chickens LLC, 1875 Goshen Road, Carthage, MS 39051.
  • What you will receive: automated alarm and system alert messages about conditions in the poultry houses, and notices when an alarm clears. These are operational messages only. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.
  • How often: message frequency varies and depends on alarm activity.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • How to stop: reply STOP to any alert at any time, or tell a farm administrator. Reply HELP for help.
  • Your information: your mobile number is used only to send these alerts. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or consent records with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Full details are in our SMS Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Your details and consent

Fill in your details, print, sign, and return the form to a farm administrator.

What happens next

A farm administrator keeps your signed form on file and adds your number to the alert list. Nobody is added without a signed form. You will then receive this confirmation message:

Pham's Chickens Alerts: You are now set up to receive farm alarm text alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

A typical alert looks like this:

Pham's Chickens Alert: House 3 HIGH TEMP - 92F (limit 88F) at 2:14 PM. Please check the house immediately. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
