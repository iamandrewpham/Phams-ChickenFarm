Farm Alarm Text Alerts

SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective September 17, 2026

1. The program

Pham's Chickens LLC ("we", "us") operates a text alert program called Pham's Chickens Alerts. The program sends automated alarm and system alert messages from the monitoring system in our poultry houses, including high or low temperature, power failure, water, and ventilation alarms, and notices when an alarm clears. Messages are operational only. We do not send marketing, promotional, or solicitation messages.

2. Who can enroll and how

The program is for employees, farm managers, and other personnel authorized by Pham's Chickens LLC. It is not open to the public. To enroll, a person completes and signs our Text Alert Consent Form. A farm administrator then adds the mobile number to the alert list and keeps the signed form on file. No number is added without a signed form. After enrollment, we send a confirmation message:

Pham's Chickens Alerts: You are now set up to receive farm alarm text alerts. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. The number of messages depends on alarm activity at the farm. Some weeks you may receive none, and during an equipment or weather event you may receive several in one day.

4. Cost

We do not charge for these messages. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your plan.

5. How to opt out

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and after that you will not receive further alerts from this number. You can also ask a farm administrator to remove your number. To rejoin, reply START or ask a farm administrator to add you again.

6. How to get help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email us at REPLACE_WITH_CONTACT_EMAIL.

7. Delivery is not guaranteed

Text alerts depend on cellular networks and other systems we do not control. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Alerts are one of several ways we monitor the poultry houses, and they do not replace on-site alarms, scheduled checks, or normal farm procedures.

8. Your responsibilities

Tell a farm administrator if your mobile number changes or if you stop using the number you enrolled, so alerts are not sent to someone else.

9. Privacy

We use your mobile number only to send these alerts. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or text message consent with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
